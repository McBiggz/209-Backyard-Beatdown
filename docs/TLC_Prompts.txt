===== 1. THE WEAPONS (attach The Viral Voice standing sprite) =====

Using the attached sprite of The Viral Voice (a 6 ft wrestler) as the size reference, in the exact same 16-bit SNES pixel-art style, outline thickness and scale, draw ONE sheet of wrestling weapons at real-life size.

Weapons are real-life size next to the wrestler. NEVER draw them small or toy-sized. For a 6 ft wrestler:
- Steel chair: grey steel folding chair. Open: the seat is at his knee height and the top of the backrest reaches his hip. Folded flat: about half his height long, as wide as his shoulders.
- Table: classic folding wrestling table, brown wood top with black metal folding legs. As long as he is tall (6 ft). Standing open, the tabletop reaches just below his belt. Folded: a flat board as long as he is tall.
- Ladder: tall silver aluminum A-frame ladder with yellow side rails and black rubber feet. Standing open it is about ONE AND A HALF TIMES his height. Closed, it is almost twice his height long.
A shorter wrestler gets the same real-size weapon, so it looks bigger on him.

Row 1 (size check, one wide frame): The Viral Voice standing, facing right, next to an open steel chair, an open table and an open ladder, all four on the same floor line.
Row 2 (table, no wrestler): 1. open table, long side facing us. 2. folded flat, lying down. 3. folded, standing up on its end, leaning slightly. 4. snapped in a V in the middle, still in one piece. 5. two broken halves lying flat with splinters.
Row 3 (ladder, no wrestler): 1. open A-frame standing. 2. closed, standing straight up. 3. closed, lying flat. 4. closed, falling over at a 45° angle.
Row 4 (chair, no wrestler): 1. open, standing, side view. 2. folded, standing upright. 3. folded, lying flat. 4. folded, badly dented in the middle.

Solid flat magenta background (#FF00FF) everywhere. No floor, ring, ropes, referee, shadows, motion lines, borders, numbers, labels or text.
